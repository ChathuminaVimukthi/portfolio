# Personal Website PRD – Engineering Growth, Leadership & Lightweight Portfolio

## 1. Vision

This personal website is a long-term reflection space documenting my growth as a software engineer evolving into a technology leader.

It is not a traditional portfolio site.

It is a **hybrid platform** combining:
- Leadership & engineering reflections (core identity)
- Real-world delivery and decision-making insights
- A lightweight technical portfolio (credibility layer)

The goal is to create a space that is:
- Authentic and reflective
- Technically credible
- Useful for both personal growth and community learning

---

## 2. Core Principles

- Authenticity over presentation
- Reflection over marketing
- Depth over quantity
- Clarity over complexity
- Long-term growth over short-term impressions
- Leadership thinking over pure execution focus

---

## 3. Goals

### Primary Goals
- Document real engineering + leadership experiences
- Share insights from:
  - Team leadership
  - Delivery ownership (DRPs, planning, execution)
  - Client / account exposure
  - Engineering decision-making under constraints
- Build a consistent writing habit (blogging)
- Create a long-term personal growth archive

### Secondary Goals
- Provide a lightweight technical portfolio for credibility
- Showcase real engineering capability (not just leadership narrative)
- Share learnings with the engineering community
- Serve as a reflection point for career growth

---

## 4. Website Positioning

The website represents:

> “The journey of an engineer evolving into a leadership role while learning how engineering, delivery, and business intersect.”

It should communicate:
- Strong technical foundation
- Increasing leadership responsibility
- Exposure to delivery and business context
- Continuous learning mindset

---

## 5. Target Audience

### Primary Audience
- Future self (reflection and growth tracking)
- Engineering peers (Tech Leads, Senior Engineers)
- Engineers transitioning into leadership roles

### Secondary Audience
- Recruiters (supporting signal only)
- Engineering Managers / Technical Leaders
- Tech community readers

---

## 6. Website Structure

## 6.1 Home (Critical Layer)

### Purpose
Provide immediate clarity of identity and credibility.

### Content
- Clear positioning statement:
  > Tech Lead focused on engineering delivery, team growth, and evolving toward business-aware leadership.

- Short summary (2–3 lines):
  - current role
  - leadership scope
  - technical stack

- Quick navigation:
  - About
  - Blog
  - Projects
  - Notes

---

## 6.2 About (Journey Layer)

### Purpose
Tell professional evolution story.

### Content
- Engineering background
- Transition into Tech Lead role
- Leadership responsibilities:
  - Team leadership
  - Delivery ownership (DRPs, planning, execution)
  - Client / account exposure
- Growth focus:
  - leadership maturity
  - communication
  - business understanding

Tone:
- honest
- reflective
- non-corporate

---

## 6.3 Blog / Insights (Core Value Layer)

### Purpose
Primary content engine of the site.

### Content Types

#### Leadership reflections
- Communication improvements
- Handling delivery risk
- Team leadership experiences

#### Engineering insights
- System design thinking
- Tradeoffs in real delivery scenarios

#### Delivery & business exposure
- DRPs and planning insights
- Margin/resource thinking (high-level reflections, not financial detail)
- Client alignment lessons

#### Interview/hiring insights
- “Lessons from 50+ technical interviews”

Tone:
- first-person
- real experiences
- grounded and reflective

---

## 6.4 Projects (Lightweight Portfolio Layer)

### Purpose
Provide technical credibility without dominating the site.

### Structure per project
- Problem statement
- Approach
- Tech stack (.NET, React, Azure, etc.)
- Key learnings
- Tradeoffs or challenges

### Content type
- Full-stack applications
- Internal tools (if shareable)
- Experiments / learning projects

---

## 6.5 Notes (Optional / Thinking Space)

### Purpose
Low-friction thoughts and evolving ideas

Content:
- short reflections
- leadership observations
- engineering patterns
- communication lessons
- ideas in progress

---

## 7. Tech Stack

### Frontend
- Next.js (React-based framework)
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion (subtle animations)

### UI / Styling
- Tailwind CSS
- ShadCN UI (optional components)
- Responsive-first design approach

### Content Management
- Markdown-based blog system
- MDX support for embedded components

### Backend (optional)
- Next.js API routes
- or serverless functions (Vercel)

### Deployment
- Vercel (primary)
- GitHub (version control)

---

## 8. Design System

## 8.1 Design Philosophy
- Minimal and calm
- Content-first experience
- No unnecessary visual complexity
- Slight personal identity (not corporate SaaS style)

---

## 8.2 Color Theme

### Light Mode
- Background: #FAFAFA
- Primary text: #111827
- Secondary text: #6B7280
- Accent: #2563EB
- Borders: #E5E7EB

### Dark Mode
- Background: #0B0F19
- Primary text: #F9FAFB
- Secondary text: #9CA3AF
- Accent: #3B82F6
- Borders: #1F2937

---

## 8.3 Typography
- Primary font: Inter
- Monospace: JetBrains Mono (code snippets)
- Emphasis:
  - large headings
  - readable spacing
  - clean hierarchy

---

## 8.4 UI Style
- Rounded corners (8–12px)
- Soft shadows (minimal use)
- High whitespace
- Subtle hover animations
- Smooth transitions

---

## 8.5 Layout Style
- Center-aligned layout
- Max width: 1100–1200px
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Simple navigation structure

---

## 9. UX Principles

- Fast loading experience
- Easy navigation between blog and projects
- Readability optimized for long-form content
- Minimal cognitive load
- Clear separation between:
  - reflection content
  - technical proof content

---

## 10. Success Metrics (Personal)

- Consistent publishing (1–2 blog posts/month)
- Clear documentation of leadership growth
- Ability to reflect on past decisions
- Improved articulation of engineering + business thinking
- Strong personal knowledge archive over time

---

## 11. Non-Goals

This website is NOT:
- A pure portfolio site
- A recruiter-optimized marketing funnel
- A certification showcase
- A flashy UI/UX showcase

---

## 12. Long-Term Vision

Over time, this website becomes:
- A leadership journal
- A documented engineering growth timeline
- A reflection of evolving technical + business thinking
- A reference point for future leadership roles
- A knowledge base for other engineers transitioning into leadership

---

## 13. Final Statement

This website represents:

> “A hybrid identity of engineer, team leader, and evolving business-aware technology leader, documenting real-world growth through experience, reflection, and technical work.”
